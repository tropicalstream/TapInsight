# TapInsight — 10-Minute Demo Script

**Talent:** Mars · **Audio note:** All YouTube playback in this demo is glitch music — any recording-audio skip blends into the genre.

---

### 0:00 — Cold open *(camera on you, glasses off)*
> "Most AI assistants live in your phone. This one lives in your line of sight."
> *(Beat.)*
> "I'm Mars. This is TapInsight. It turns a regular pair of AR glasses into something that sees what you see, hears what you ask, and actually does the thing. Ten minutes, no slides."

### 0:30 — Glasses on, HUD reveal
> "Glasses on. No menu. No icons. Just this."
> *(Glance up to the HUD.)*
> "Time. Weather. Air quality. My next calendar event. Today's top headlines — always updating, always hands-free. Everything a smartwatch tries to be, floating in front of me."

### 1:10 — First voice moment *(tap to start Gemini Live)*
**Say to glasses:** *"Hey Gemini — what's on my calendar today and how's the weather looking?"*
> "That's Gemini 3.1 Flash Live. Streaming, conversational, sub-second. It sees the HUD, my calendar, my location — and it stops talking the instant I start."

### 2:00 — Discover-and-describe *(new YouTube flow)*
**Say:** *"Find me some good glitch music videos."*
> "Notice what just happened — it's describing the videos to me first. Titles, artists, one line each. Nothing on the screen yet. It recommends; I decide."
> *(After it finishes.)*
**Say:** *"Send me the list."*
> "Now the picker lands on my glasses. Tap a row to play that one. Red button plays the whole topic as a playlist."

### 3:15 — Play a track + audio visualizer
*(Tap a glitch track. Enable the visualizer.)*
> "Playing in the AR browser. And because music deserves a stage — TapInsight ships ten visualizer themes. Meditative breathing, Tron light-wall, graphic equalizer, Close Encounters, atomic pulse. Reactive to whatever's playing. This is sit-back mode."

### 4:15 — 3D maps + fly-over *(our new landmark feature)*
**Say:** *"Show me a 3D map of the Seattle Space Needle."*
> "Photorealistic 3D, straight from Google. Framed on the landmark, not some random midpoint. And now — "
> *(Tap the orange fly-over button.)*
> "A cinematic orbit. Same API Google Earth uses. Zero effort."

### 5:30 — Directions that sound like a human
**Say:** *"Navigate me to the nearest pour-over coffee shop."*
> "Places search and Routes, tied together. It reads me the drive time, the walk time, the parking notes. All spoken. All glanceable."

### 6:30 — TapClaw, the personal agent
> "Gemini is the brain. TapClaw is the hands."
**Say:** *"TapClaw, email me this video."*
> "It can't invent the URL — it's grounded to the real video I'm watching. TapClaw writes the email, sends it, and pings the HUD when it's done."

### 7:30 — Research on demand
**Say:** *"Research the history of glitch music."*
> "Full multi-source research brief. Read aloud in a voice I picked. Saved to the reader for later."

### 8:15 — Adapts to you
> "One more thing that matters. I can dim the brightness, reposition the HUD, swap the readout voice — all from the companion app or by voice. Glasses should adapt to me, not the other way around."

### 9:00 — Close
> "That's TapInsight. Gemini Live in your eyeline, two dozen tools behind it, and an AR browser so anything it finds becomes something you can actually use."
> *(Beat.)*
> "Install it, plug in a key, and put the future on your face."

---

**Run time:** ≈ 9 min 45 s · **Cue cards:** every voice command is on its own line, bolded — pause, then say it. Keep glitch music as the only YouTube content throughout.
